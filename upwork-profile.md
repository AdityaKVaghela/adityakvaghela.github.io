## 5. Profile settings worth checking

- **Hourly rate** — your call, but set it deliberately. Senior iOS/Flutter with
  5 years and App Store credits sits well above entry pricing.
- **Availability** — "More than 30 hrs/week" if you want full-time contracts.
- **Languages** — English (Fluent), Hindi, Gujarati. Upwork *does* rank on this,
  which is why it belongs here even though it came off the portfolio site.
- **Video introduction** — Upwork boosts profiles that have one. 30 seconds:
  name, 5 years mobile, the 70%/50%/1000+ numbers, what you want to work on.
- **Specialized profile** — create one for "Mobile Development" so you appear in
  that category's talent search.
- **Portfolio link** — add https://adityakvaghela.github.io in the profile
  overview and as a project link.
